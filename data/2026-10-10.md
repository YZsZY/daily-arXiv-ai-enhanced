### [1] [Connected Self Forcing: Beyond Local Learning in Video Autoregression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12156)
*Dongbin Zhang,Chaoda Zheng,Kangjie Chen,Xiangyu Li,Shijia Chen,Jinhao Deng,Yuqi Zhang,Guangfeng Jiang,Hongbin Lin,Choo Sin Wai,Minqi Wang,Puyi Wang,Jingye Zhang,Yu Zhang,Xianming Liu,Boyang Wang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出Connected Self Forcing训练框架，通过重连自回归视频生成中各chunk之间的梯度路径，并采用shortcut gradient replay实现内存高效训练，在不改变推理过程的前提下提升了长时程视频的视觉质量与时间一致性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有Self Forcing方法通过自生成历史的自rollout训练缓解曝光偏差，但为控制内存会detach历史KV缓存，这虽然保留了chunk间的前向依赖，却切断了反向梯度路径，导致后续预测无法反馈指导早期上下文的生成。因此作者希望重新连接跨chunk的梯度路径以提升生成质量。

Method: 提出Connected Self Forcing训练框架：不仅让梯度经过历史KV写入，还让梯度通过生成的latent流入产生它们的计算过程，从而将早期上下文的生成与其在后续预测中的使用关联起来。为实现内存高效训练，开发了shortcut gradient replay，无需保留完整rollout计算图即可恢复跨chunk梯度。该方法与distribution matching distillation结合，使历史chunk同时接受直接监督和其对后续生成贡献的训练信号。

Result: 在自回归视频生成任务上的实验表明，长时程视觉质量和时间一致性均有提升，且推理过程保持不变。

Conclusion: 通过重连自回归chunk间的梯度路径并采用内存高效的梯度重放，Connected Self Forcing能够在推理不变的情况下改善长视频生成的视觉质量与时间一致性。

Abstract: To stream long videos while maintaining visual quality and temporal consistency, Self Forcing mitigates exposure bias through self-rollout training on self-generated histories with key-value (KV) caching. To keep memory manageable, it detaches historical caches, preserving forward dependencies between chunks but severing the backward gradient paths. We introduce Connected Self Forcing, a training framework that reconnects gradient paths across autoregressive chunks, allowing feedback from later predictions to guide how earlier context is generated. These connections go beyond historical KV-writing: gradients pass through generated latents into the computations that produced them, linking the generation of earlier context to its use in later predictions. To make this connected training memory-efficient, we develop shortcut gradient replay, which recovers cross-chunk gradients without retaining the full rollout computation graph. Integrated with distribution matching distillation, Connected Self Forcing trains historical chunks according to both their direct supervision and their contribution to subsequent generation. Experiments on autoregressive video generation show improvements in long-horizon visual quality and temporal consistency, without changing the inference procedure.

### [2] [DVD: Dynamic Vector Decoding for Efficient MLLM-based Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12266)
*Jinghua Hou,Zhe Liu,Hengshuang Zhao*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出动态向量解码方法DVD，将2D/3D感知任务统一表示为1D向量序列并映射为紧凑离散token，通过轻量反tokenizer与MLLM无缝集成，在多个基准上实现优越性能并显著降低token开销与推理延迟。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有多模态大模型感知方法主要依赖文本坐标表示，存在token开销过大的问题；或采用固定范围量化，受到范围和精度限制，尤其难以应对空间范围无界且定位精度要求高的3D场景。因此需要一种统一且高效的感知表示方法。

Method: 提出动态向量解码方法DVD：首先将多样的感知表示（2D检测框、2D掩码、3D检测框）转化为1D向量序列，再映射到高维空间中的紧凑离散token；随后通过一个轻量级反tokenizer将输出的token解码回原始2D和3D感知表示，实现与MLLM的无缝集成。

Result: 在RefCOCO系列、SUN-RGBD、KITTI、Hypersim、nuScenes等2D和3D感知基准上的大量实验表明，DVD在2D和3D任务上均取得优越性能，同时显著减少token开销并降低推理延迟。

Conclusion: DVD为将感知能力集成到MLLM中提供了一个高效且通用的框架，克服了现有方法的固有局限。

Abstract: Multimodal large language models have made remarkable progress in bridging vision and language, facilitating various perception tasks essential for human-machine interaction, robotics, and autonomous driving. However, existing MLLM-based perception methods predominantly rely on text-based coordinate representation, which suffers from excessive token overhead, or fixed-range quantization, which suffers from range and precision constraints, especially for 3D domains with unbounded spatial range and high localization accuracy requirements. To address these challenges, we propose a dynamic vector decoding method named DVD, which unifies the representation of 2D and 3D perception tasks. Specifically, we first transform diverse perceptual representation (i.e., 2D bounding boxes, 2D masks, and 3D bounding boxes) into 1D vector sequences, which are then mapped to compact discrete tokens in the high-dimensional space. Then, a lightweight de-tokenizer enables seamless integration with MLLMs by decoding output tokens back to original 2D and 3D perceptual representations. Extensive experiments on 2D and 3D perception benchmarks including RefCOCO series, SUN-RGBD, KITTI, Hypersim, nuScenes demonstrate that DVD achieves superior performance in 2D and 3D tasks and reduces significantly the token overhead and inference latency. DVD provides an efficient and general framework for integrating perception capabilities into MLLMs, overcoming the inherent limitations of existing methods.

### [3] [Slot3R: Set-Associative Spatial Memory for Streaming 3D Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12282)
*Xiyuan Zhang,Yanming Yang,Kaiyuan Xu,Ruibo Li,Chi Zhang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 该论文提出Slot3R，一种免训练、集合关联式的流式3D重建改进方法，通过"位置决定地址而非是否合并"的原则，让多个状态在同一空间地址共存，在保持Point3R主干冻结的情况下大幅提升重建精度与显存效率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 流式3D重建需在处理不断扩张场景时保留每一帧的证据。空间记忆按重建的3D位置组织历史，天然适合该任务。然而Point3R将空间邻近性同时用于"关联新观测与已有记忆条目"和"决定是否融合"，混淆了"共同定位"与"状态同一性"。由于指针(pointer)是对图像块的摘要，邻近的指针可能编码不同的表面、视角或可见性条件，直接平均会在后续帧尚未消歧前就破坏互补证据。

Method: 作者主张位置应决定"地址"而非"是否必须合并"。Slot3R将这一原则实现为一种免训练、集合关联式的改造方案，允许在同一共享地址上共存多个状态，同时保持预训练的Point3R主干完全冻结。此外，引入有界稀疏读出(bounded sparse readout)，将持久化存储与逐帧解码器访问进一步解耦。

Result: 在300-500采样帧设置下，Slot3R将Point3R的点云精度误差(Acc)在7Scenes上降低57.1%-63.1%，在NeuralRGBD上降低64.0%-72.0%；在全部三个位姿基准上降低了Sim(3)对齐的绝对轨迹误差(ATE)；在视频深度估计上保持竞争力。在600至1000采样帧的所有评估设置下，以约19 FPS完成运行，而Point3R和InfiniteVGGT在800帧及以上出现显存溢出(OOM)。

Conclusion: Slot3R证明将空间地址与状态融合决策解耦的有效性，在不重新训练主干的前提下显著提升流式3D重建的精度、位姿估计与可扩展性，并支持更长序列的在线处理。

Abstract: Streaming 3D reconstruction must preserve evidence from each frame while processing an expanding scene online. Spatial memory is a natural fit because it organizes history by reconstructed 3D location. Yet Point3R uses spatial proximity both to associate a new observation with an existing memory entry and to decide whether to fuse it, conflating co-location with state identity. Because pointers summarize image patches, nearby pointers may encode distinct surfaces, viewpoints, or visibility conditions; averaging them can destroy complementary evidence before later frames disambiguate it. We argue that location should determine address, not whether observations must merge. Slot3R realizes this principle as a training-free, set-associative retrofit that lets multiple states coexist at a shared address while keeping the pretrained Point3R backbone frozen. A bounded sparse readout further decouples persistent storage from per-frame decoder access. At 300-500 sampled frames, Slot3R reduces Point3R's point-cloud accuracy error (Acc) by 57.1%-63.1% on 7Scenes and 64.0%-72.0% on NeuralRGBD, lowers Sim(3)-aligned absolute trajectory error (ATE) on all three pose benchmarks, and remains competitive on video-depth estimation. It completes all evaluated settings from 600 to 1000 sampled frames at about 19 FPS under the same protocol, whereas Point3R and InfiniteVGGT run out of memory at 800 frames and beyond.

### [4] [Multi-Agent Egocentric World Model with Fine-Grained Embodied Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12299)

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出多智能体自我中心世界模型（ME-World），将多智能体自我中心世界建模定义为共享世界中多个智能体通过细粒度动作交互的同步自我流生成，并在真实与合成数据上验证其优越性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有自我中心世界模型多聚焦单一智能体，而真实具身场景常涉及多智能体在共享环境中交互。现有多智能体世界模型依赖粗粒度动作（如移动、相机控制、离散指令），细粒度具身交互尚待探索。该任务要求跨视角动作一致性、共享环境一致性以及交互引发状态更新的一致性传播。

Method: 提出ME-World，在共享token序列中联合去噪多个自我流，以所有智能体的目标视角姿态为条件，并利用共享环境记忆进行生成接地。在真实与合成多智能体数据上训练与评估，并提出共享世界一致性指标，涵盖环境、更新与身份一致性。

Result: 实验表明，ME-World在共享世界一致性、动作控制、身份保持和视频质量方面均优于现有方法。

Conclusion: ME-World为多智能体细粒度交互的自我中心世界建模提供了有效方案，能更好保持共享世界一致性与身份一致性，并提升生成质量。

Abstract: Egocentric world models predict first-person observations conditioned on an agent's actions, but most focus on a single agent. Real embodied settings often involve multiple agents that act and interact within a shared environment. Existing multi-agent world models rely on coarse actions like locomotion, camera control, or discrete commands, leaving fine-grained embodied interactions underexplored. We formulate multi-agent egocentric world modeling as synchronized ego-stream generation for multiple agents interacting through fine-grained actions in a shared world. This requires cross-view action consistency, shared-environment consistency, and consistent propagation of interaction-induced state updates. We propose Multi-agent Egocentric World Model (ME-World), which jointly denoises multiple ego streams in a shared token sequence, conditions each stream on all agents' target-view poses, and grounds generation with shared environment memory. We train and evaluate on real and synthetic multi-agent data and introduce shared-world consistency metrics for environment, update, and identity consistency. Experiments show ME-World improves shared-world consistency, action control, identity preservation, and video quality over existing methods.

### [5] [Reasoning-Informed Visual Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12343)
*Xue Yang,Peiyuan Zhang,Yilun Zhu,Qihao Yang,Mingxin Liu,Xiangyu Zhao,Ziqian Fan,Zhaokai Wang,Yan Li,Yifan Yang,Xu Yang,Xiaosong Jia,Yue Zhou,Zhihang Zhong,Junchi Yan*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出RISEBench及扩展版RISEBench++，首个评估"推理引导视觉编辑"(RISE)的基准，涵盖六大推理维度、12子类、65细粒度任务，1000条中英双语人工标注测试用例，并引入免训练智能体框架RISE-Agent，评估58种方法后发现推理式视觉编辑仍面临重大挑战。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大型多模态模型在视觉理解与生成上进展显著，但在视觉编辑方面仍面临挑战，尤其是遵循复杂指令、保持外观一致性和支持灵活输入格式。为研究这一空白，作者构建了专门评估推理引导视觉编辑的基准。

Method: 构建RISEBench及RISEBench++基准：分类体系扩展为六种推理维度（时序、因果、空间、逻辑、反事实及融合多推理类型的混合推理），细分为12子类与65任务类型；输入格式扩展至多图像条件；规模扩大至1000条中英双语人工标注用例。改进评估框架，从指令推理、外观一致性和视觉合理性三方面评估，结合人类评审与LMM-as-a-judge。同时提出免训练智能体框架RISE-Agent，融合推理驱动规划、工具增强执行和验证器引导优化。

Result: 评估了58种视觉编辑方法（34个开源、19个闭源、5个智能体方法）。结果显示推理式视觉编辑挑战巨大，最强方法GPT-Image-2.5 Sunburst仅达56.6%准确率。RISE-Agent在多样RISE任务上超越多数现有强方法。

Abstract: Large Multi-modality Models (LMMs) have made significant progress in visual understanding and generation, but still face challenges in visual editing, particularly in following complex instructions, preserving appearance consistency, and supporting flexible input formats. To study this gap, we introduce RISEBench, the first benchmark for evaluating Reasoning-Informed viSual Editing (RISE), and extend it to RISEBench++, a more comprehensive and fine-grained benchmark for this emerging task. RISEBench++ extends the taxonomy into a hierarchical scheme spanning six reasoning dimensions: Temporal, Causal, Spatial, Logical, and Counterfactual Reasoning, together with Hybrid Reasoning integrating multiple reasoning types across multi-turn edits. These dimensions are further decomposed into 12 subcategories and 65 fine-grained task types. We expand input formats to include multi-image conditioning and scale the benchmark to 1000 human-annotated test cases, released in English and Chinese. We also improve our evaluation framework, assessing Instruction Reasoning, Appearance Consistency, and Visual Plausibility with human judges and an LMM-as-a-judge approach for more reliable and calibrated judgements. Beyond benchmarking, we introduce RISE-Agent, a training-free agentic framework integrating reasoning-driven planning, tool-augmented execution, and verifier-guided refinement, outperforming most strong existing approaches across diverse RISE tasks. We evaluate 58 visual editing approaches, including 34 open-source models, 19 closed-source models, and 5 agentic methods. The results reveal substantial challenges in reasoning-based visual editing, with even the strongest evaluated approach, GPT-Image-2.5 Sunburst, achieving only 56.6% accuracy. RISEBench++ highlights the limitations of contemporary editing models, provides insights, and indicates future directions for reasoning-aware visual editing.

### [6] [AgentGarten: Code Worlds for Evolving Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12374)
*Jiawei Chi,Shangchen Miao,Zhiyuan Shi,Kailu Wu,Hanyang Wang,Weiliang Chen,Qiyu Dai,Jinshan Ren,Jun Gao,Mingsheng Long,Yueqi Duan,Jiangran Lyu,Jialong Wu,Fangfu Liu*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: AgentGarten 是一个将模拟器与游戏引擎通过共享神经渲染器耦合的框架，用于构建实时交互式虚拟环境，使智能体能在真实且一致的多样化世界中学习。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 智能体通过探索与交互学习，其学习能力受限于所训练环境的品质。理想环境需同时具备忠实性（一致的状态、规则与动力学）和真实性（符合真实世界视觉分布的观测），但在多样化世界中兼顾二者仍是瓶颈。

Method: 框架采用共享神经渲染器耦合模拟后端与游戏引擎：模拟后端维护持久世界状态并执行程序定义的交互规则，渲染器从统一接口导出的结构化条件生成视觉观测。神经渲染器由预训练视频模型适配几何条件，并通过提出的 Adversarial Forcing 蒸馏，再针对实时交互优化推理。Adversarial Forcing 通过精确重放使历史预填充可微，让后续预测损失回传以更新渲染器对先前观测的编码，并加入真实数据对抗监督提升视觉质量。智能体通过视觉观测感知世界、实时交互，并将每轮经验蒸馏为可供后续智能体继承与改进的 playbook。

Result: 实证研究表明学习效率大幅提升：智能体仅需 4 轮即可学习，而传统强化学习对照需数百万轮。由于新世界可写成代码并通过同一接口渲染，环境数量与难度可与智能体共同扩展。

Conclusion: AgentGarten 让环境在数量与难度上随智能体一同扩展，是迈向通过交互经验持续进化智能体的一步。

Abstract: Interactive virtual worlds allow agents to learn through exploration and interaction. What agents can learn is bounded by the environments they practice in, which must be faithful, with consistent state, rules, and dynamics, and realistic, with observations that follow the real-world visual distributions. To build the neural renderer, we adapt a pretrained video model to geometry conditions, distill it with our proposed Adversarial Forcing, and optimize inference for real-time interaction. Adversarial Forcing makes history prefilling differentiable through exact replay, so that losses on later predictions update how the renderer encodes prior observations, and adds real-data adversarial supervision to improve its visual quality. In AgentGarten, agents perceive the world through visual observations, interact with it in real time, and improve by distilling each round of experience into playbooks that subsequent agents inherit and refine. Our empirical study demonstrates a substantial gain in learning efficiency, with agents learning from just 4 rounds compared with millions for a conventional reinforcement learning counterpart. As new worlds can be written as code and rendered through the same interface, environments can scale in both number and difficulty alongside their agents, a step toward agents that keep evolving through interactive experience.
