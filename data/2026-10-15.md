### [1] [Action Jets: Learning the Local Shape of the Action-conditioned Data Manifold](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19007)
*Anton Arapin,Chahyon Ku,Bernadette Bucher*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出 Action Jet：在固定状态下，用一次网络推理估计低维动作流形的局部形状（三阶泰勒多项式），从而对任意多个候选动作快速打分，比原生成器快 300 倍以上且短期预测更准。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 动作条件世界模型维度高、计算昂贵，限制了规划器可评估的候选动作数量；而短视界规划只需当前状态附近的局部信息，因此可以避免重复的完整生成推理。

Method: 观察到固定状态下由低维动作（相机移动或末端执行器指令）可达的帧位于低维流形上；训练网络估计每个图像区域沿每个动作轴单位位移的运动，并结合由轨迹数据拟合的修正头，估计该流形区域的三阶泰勒多项式；给出有效半径的闭式解；每个状态只需一次推理即可遍历该局部流形近似，对任意数量候选动作打分。

Result: 单次提取对 16 个候选动作打分比两个源生成器快 300 倍以上，且候选数越多优势越大；在 BridgeData V2 真实 7-DoF 操作基准上与 IRASim、WorldGym 对比，在 DL3DV 场景上基于 DepthSplat、LagerNVS 的新视角生成与 FrameCrafter 对比；在短期界下预测留出帧比提供切空间的 IRASim 更准，计算量降低 100 倍以上。

Conclusion: 仅切向由生成器提供、其余高阶项由数据学习，即可高效且更准确地刻画动作条件数据流形的局部几何，为短视界世界模型规划提供大幅加速的候选动作评估方案。

Abstract: World models are a powerful means of reasoning and planning, but many of these action-conditioned predictive models are high dimensional and computationally expensive. These challenges limit the number of action candidates that can be evaluated by a planner. We show that this cost is avoidable for planners over short horizons, which only require local information with respect to the current state. For a fixed state, the frames reachable by varying a low-dimensional action (a camera movement or an end-effector command) lie on a low-dimensional manifold. We estimate the local shape of that manifold directly via a network trained to estimate how each image region moves per unit of each action axis supervised by data collected from samples by the generator. A correction head fit to trajectory data estimates the shape of this manifold region up to a third-order Taylor polynomial. We call this network estimating the Taylor polynomial describing the manifold shape an Action Jet and compute its radius of validity in closed form. By traversing this approximation of the local manifold shape, we only have to perform inference once per state for any number of candidate actions. Scoring sixteen candidates from a single extraction is over 300x faster than with either source generator, and this gap widens with the number of candidates a planner evaluates. We measure against state-of-the-art action-conditioned generators, IRASim and WorldGym on BridgeData V2, a real 7-DoF manipulation benchmark, and FrameCrafter on DL3DV scenes, where we chart from the novel-view generators DepthSplat and LagerNVS. Because only the tangent comes from the generator and every other term from the data, the Action Jet also predicts held-out frames more accurately than IRASim, the generator that supplied its tangent, at short horizons and at over 100x less compute.

Result: 在RE10K、ACID、DL3DV上，MFGS相对"先生成后压缩"流程按BD-rate实现99.76-99.86%码率节省，总GMACs降低52-55%，端到端约12倍加速。

Conclusion: 直接压缩多视图特征而非显式高斯表示，可端到端联合建模几何与外观冗余，显著提升率失真性能与效率，并支持灵活的渲染预算调节。

Abstract: Feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) methods enable efficient reconstruction of 3D scenes from sparse multi-view images in a single forward pass, without scene-specific optimization. However, existing feed-forward scene-coding pipelines first generate a complete Gaussian representation and then encode its geometry and appearance attributes separately, often involving non-differentiable operations. The resulting 'generate-then-compress' pipeline requires complex attribute-specific codec designs and limits rate-distortion performance by restricting the exploitation of cross-attribute redundancy. We propose multi-view feature coding for Gaussian splatting (MFGS), an end-to-end feed-forward framework for scene coding. Instead of compressing an explicit Gaussian representation, MFGS extracts multi-view features from the input images and encodes them into a compact bitstream. Through end-to-end rate-distortion training, MFGS learns compact multi-view features that jointly represent geometry and appearance, enabling the receiver to predict Gaussian attributes from the decoded features. We further introduce importance-guided Gaussian selection to accommodate different rendering budgets, allowing the receiver to adjust the number of Gaussians used for rendering from the same bitstream. Across RE10K, ACID, and DL3DV, MFGS achieves 99.76-99.86% bitrate savings over the generate-then-compress pipeline, as measured by BD-rate, while reducing total GMACs by 52-55% and providing an approximately 12 times end-to-end speedup.
