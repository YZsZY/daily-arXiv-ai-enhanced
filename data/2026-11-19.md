Abstract: We present the Autoregressive View Model (AVM), which generates a scene as a stream of views without constructing an explicit 3D representation. Through a unified in-context interface, AVM conditions on images or videos to generate short clips along a requested camera path. Visual context grounds reconstruction and interpolation, while a learned prior enables extrapolation into unseen regions. AVM retrieves relevant views from inputs and generated history to form a bounded active context. We fine-tune a pretrained bidirectional video diffusion transformer with flow matching in an N-in-1-out formulation. We then tune a causal model on its own rollouts to improve photorealism, reduce denoising steps, and limit long-horizon drift. We scale training to approximately 8M scenes and video clips. We evaluate generation capacity and scene persistence across benchmarks for camera control, text control, visual quality, dynamics, sparse and 4D reconstruction, and long-horizon consistency. The results demonstrate that AVM adapts to diverse input and output settings through one in-context interface.

Abstract: Streaming 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) allows for online reconstruction of dynamic scenes from multi-view videos for Free Viewpoint Video (FVV). Current time and storage efficient methods rely on selecting a subset of anchor Gaussians and neighborhood interpolation for fast streamable 3DGS. However, these methods can overlook object-specific motion and introduce inconsistencies near object boundaries. We propose OCLDA-GS, an object-centric streaming framework that dynamically reallocates object and motion-aware Gaussian anchors to allocate anchor Gaussian support for objects undergoing large motion while at the same time, restricting the gaussian neighborhood within object groups for more precise object boundaries. Furthermore, it estimates object-wise rigid transformations (e.g. a hand moving) and leverages these estimates to learn per-anchor local deformation correction for small deformations (e.g. wrinkles on a shirt). Then, we conduct experiments on three dynamic multi-view video datasets and demonstrate improvements over the previous baselines alongside improved quality between refinement timesteps.
